# 3. Competitor audit

Use with `PROMPT.md` and the completed brief.

## Inputs

Your ad copy and landing-page capture, plus 2–5 competitor ad/page captures. Each should include a source URL or filename, capture date, country/language, and device where known. Use supplied captures first. Public research is optional only when I request it; no competitor-account login is needed.

## Copy-paste task

Build an evidence-backed comparison of our message and the supplied competitors. For each, extract: audience, problem, hook, offer, proof, call to action, friction, and ad-to-page consistency. Cite the capture section for each observed claim. Separate a competitor's claim from a verified fact. Do not invent spend, CTR, conversion rate, targeting, or profitability.

Identify three underused angles that our approved facts can support. For each angle, give the audience, original message, supporting proof from our own materials, required page changes, proposed test metric, and why it may matter. Do not copy competitor sentences or imply their claims apply to us. If an attractive angle needs proof we lack, label it a research idea, not ready-to-run copy.

Capture dates and regional personalization limit the comparison. Screenshots may omit footers, pricing conditions, consent overlays, or mobile content. Do not infer that something is absent from the live site simply because a partial capture does not show it.

## Deliverables and review

- `03-competitor-audit.md`: comparison matrix, evidence, unknowns, three test briefs.
- Source index with capture date and scope.

Success means a testable, original positioning choice grounded in our offer. It does not mean claiming that an observed competitor ad performs well.
