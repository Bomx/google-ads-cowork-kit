# 5. Ad-to-page match

Use with `PROMPT.md` and the completed brief.

## Inputs

Ad IDs or stable labels, final URLs, copy, and a captured landing page for each destination. Include visible hero text, offer, CTA, form fields, pricing/conditions, and mobile screenshots where possible. Record capture date and scope. No analytics access is required.

## Copy-paste task

For each ad, compare its concrete promise to what the supplied page evidence supports. Check audience, product/service, location, price/discount, timing, CTA, proof, and important conditions. Label each promise supported, contradicted, not visible in supplied capture, or unknown. Quote only short evidence snippets and cite the capture section.

Prioritize contradictions that could mislead a visitor before cosmetic differences. Separate a mismatch from an unproven hypothesis. A missing fact in a partial screenshot is not proof the live page lacks it. Flag redirects, forms, speed, accessibility, tracking, and mobile behavior as not tested unless actual evidence was supplied.

Propose the smallest accurate correction: an ad revision, a page clarification supported by the brief, or a request for missing proof. For each correction state owner, effort estimate with uncertainty, affected promise, risk, and the metric to monitor after approval. Never invent a performance lift.

## Deliverables and review

- `05-ad-page-match.md`: promise/evidence matrix, severity, recommended fixes, untested items.
- Decision register entries with the exact wording or page section to review.

Do not edit the website or account. Legal, regulated, guarantee, and price claims need the business owner's review before publication.
