# Northstar CRM: synthetic teaching account

Everything in this folder is fictional. Names, claims, URLs, IDs, and results are teaching material, not a real customer or a case study.

- Business: subscription CRM for small agencies. Agency pipeline and follow-up workspace.
- Market/language: United States, English.
- Currency: USD. Account timezone: America/New_York.
- Campaigns: Search only. C100 Agency CRM, C200 Brand. No shared budgets in this example.
- Period: November 1–6, 2026 inclusive, six complete days. Previous: October 26–31, 2026, also six days, but different weekday composition. Not a full calendar week.
- Conversion: demo-request submission, one primary conversion metric. Fractional attribution can occur. Not verified revenue or qualified sales.
- Conversion lag: usually up to seven days. Recent conversions may be incomplete. Tracking health not independently verified.
- Target CPA: none supplied. Do not infer profitability. Review threshold: USD 20 with zero reported conversions.
- Protected terms: Northstar CRM; CRM for agencies; agency pipeline; demo; free demo. No free downloadable spreadsheet product; no jobs or courses.
- Approved facts for teaching copy: agency-specific CRM, pipeline tracking, follow-up organization, team collaboration, demo available. No verified price, discount, trial length, guarantee, certification, or outcome statistic.
- Monthly plans: C100 USD 6,000; C200 USD 1,500. Teaching plans only. Actual daily settings and bid strategy not supplied.
- Capture files: `page-evidence.md` with own page, two fictional competitors, and a legacy ad.
- `keywords.csv`: selected six-keyword subset, not whole-account totals.
- `search-terms.csv`: selected five-query subset; overlaps keyword and campaign spend. Do not add these reports.
- `campaign-performance.csv`: full two-campaign teaching totals for each period, no segmentation.
- `budget-plan.csv`: full Nov 1–6 MTD campaign spend.
- `existing-negatives.csv`: illustrative snapshot for the supplied scope only.
- `change-log.csv`: one implemented copy edit and one unapproved proposal.
- Scope: run all seven as a demonstration; local files and drafts only. Reviewer role: account owner. No actual approvals exist.
